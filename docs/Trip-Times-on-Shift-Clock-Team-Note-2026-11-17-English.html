Trip times are now read on the shift clock

Vehicle Log Book (VLB) trip cards · Trip Timing & Gap Report · Loading Equipment-wise report · 17 November 2026

1. What was wrong

On the Trip Timing report, a Shift C card of Dhoptala OB (2nd) for 01-11-26 showed one trip at 04:03 and another at 12:50 → 13:37. 12:50 looks like a Shift A time, so it seemed that Shift A trips had mixed into Shift C.

They had not. The driver wrote the time on a 12-hour clock: on a night card "12:50" means 00:50, just after midnight. Trip no. 8 at 00:50 comes before trip no. 12 at 04:03, so the order on the card agrees. The app was copying the time exactly as written, so the report printed 12:50.

2. The new rule

The shift of every card is fixed (it is locked in the batch). So every time written on the card is read on that shift's clock. A written hour can mean two clock times, for example 2:30 can be 02:30 or 14:30. The app keeps the one that falls inside the shift.

The hours come from each site's Shift Master (for example Majri Shift C is 20:00 – 04:00), so every site is read on its own clock. A trip may start up to 1 hour before the shift or run up to 2 hours after it; those times are still accepted. On 12-hour Day and Night shifts a time clearly written on the 24-hour clock (for example 20:30) is kept as written.

3. What you will see

  • Saving a card (manual entry or scan auto-save): trip times are saved on the shift clock. The time as written stays on the trip row.
  • Trip Timing & Gap Report and Loading Equipment-wise: already saved cards are read the same way, so old dates are corrected too. Nothing in the saved cards is changed by the reports.
  • Step 5 (the delayed trips) has a new last column As written on card, for example "Loading 12:50 · Unloading 13:37", beside the times read (00:50 → 01:37).
  • Step 6 (the trip card) says it in the Remark column: "Written 12:50 on the card, read as 00:50 on the Shift C clock".
  • Minutes are now correct. A trip written 12:50 → 1:37 used to work out as more than 12 hours and was shown as Not judged. It is now 47 minutes. So some delay counts on Shift B and Shift C may go up a little: these are real delays that were hidden before.

4. "Time outside shift hours"

If a time fits the shift neither way (for example 7:30 on a Shift C card), the app keeps it as written and shows "Time outside Shift C hours (21:00–05:00): check the card, not judged". That trip is not counted as a delay. It usually means a wrong time on the card or the wrong shift ticked.

5. What the team should do

  1. Tick the correct shift on every trip card. The app now trusts the shift to read the times.
  2. Writing times on the 24-hour clock (21:30, 00:50) is best, but a 12-hour time is now read correctly.
  3. When the report shows "Time outside shift hours", open the card, check the time and the shift against the paper card, and correct it.
  4. Report any trip that still looks like it belongs to another shift, with the book no. and trip no.
